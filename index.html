KidneyWise

An NHS clinical AI platform for kidney-cancer prehabilitation, built end to end as sole engineer.

A full-stack web and Android app in partnership with Hywel Dda University Health Board (NHS Wales), UWTSD and Aberystwyth University. At its centre is an AI health chatbot: a retrieval pipeline over NHS clinical content, with the language model run on-premise so patient data never leaves the environment, supporting UK GDPR and NHS information governance. Around it sits real-time clinician-patient messaging, longitudinal heart-rate charts, a guided exercise programme, and role-based access with a safety filter that blocks out-of-scope medical questions.
